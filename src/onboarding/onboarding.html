Welcome to Enter Control

Stop sending half-finished messages. Enter Control swaps Enter and Ctrl+Enter on sites you choose — Enter inserts a new line, Ctrl+Enter sends.

Getting started

1. Go to a site you want to configure Open Google Chat, Teams, or any messaging app where you want the key swap active.
2. Click the Enter Control icon in your toolbar If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece icon and pin Enter Control.
3. Click "Add current site" Chrome will ask you to confirm access to that domain. After you approve, the swap is active immediately — no reload needed.

Permissions

Enter Control requests access only to sites you explicitly add. It never reads your messages or any page content — it only intercepts Enter key events inside text inputs.

Managing sites

Open the extension popup anytime to toggle individual sites on/off or remove them entirely. Removing a site also revokes its host permission.
